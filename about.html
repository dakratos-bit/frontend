Our Story

Baked fresh,
right here in Benin City

Everything you'll find on our menu is made by hand, the same day, in Benin City.

How it started

The Baker NG began with a simple goal: to bring genuinely fresh, made-from-scratch cakes and banana bread to homes and events across Benin City. What started as small orders for friends and family grew into a trusted bakery that locals now turn to for birthdays, celebrations, and everyday cravings.

What we're known for

As a dedicated cake and banana bread vendor in Benin City, we focus on doing a few things really well rather than everything at once. Our banana bread is baked fresh daily using real, ripe bananas — no shortcuts. Our cakes are made to order, whether it's a simple loaf for the weekend or a custom cake for a big celebration.

Why customers choose us

  • Fresh, made-to-order bakes — nothing sits around for days
  • Fast pickup and delivery across Benin City
  • Simple online ordering, so you can order in minutes from your phone
  • A menu built around quality over quantity

Visit or order online

Whether you're after a cake for a special occasion or just craving our banana bread, The Baker NG delivers across Benin City and offers pickup for those nearby. Browse our menu and place your order online — it only takes a few minutes.
